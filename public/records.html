Record type 04 — Extended Linear Address

Sets the upper 16 bits for following data records.

Why it exists. A data record only carries a 16-bit address, but flash often lives in a 32-bit address space. Type 04 supplies the missing upper half.

02 0000 04 0800 F2

0x0800 << 16 = 0x08000000. The next data record at low address 0x0000 becomes absolute address 0x08000000.
